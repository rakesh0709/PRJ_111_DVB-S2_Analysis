Terms & Conditions / Prototype Scope Notice

1. Academic & Research Prototype Scope

The PRJ_111 DVB-S2 Receiver Output Stream Analyzer is an academic engineering prototype developed for Review-2 milestones. It operates exclusively on post-demodulator digital output captures (MPEG-TS, GSE, and DVB-S2 BBFrame).

2. Data Handling & Local Execution

Uploaded captures are processed locally by the application and may be temporarily staged on the local machine for analysis. No external cloud processing is required.

3. Domain Boundary & Epistemic Guards

The software evaluates digital framing syntax and anomaly statistics. It does not infer physical RF characteristics (such as SNR, Eb/N0, C/N, BER, carrier frequency drift, rain fade, or hardware failure) from digital stream indicators.

4. Verification Scope

Stream health checks represent selected stream integrity indicators inspired by ETSI TR 101 290 principles and do not constitute formal ETSI certification.
